A short history

Made for lingering.

Avenue started with a simple idea: a place where the coffee is hot, the booths are comfy, and nobody rushes you out the door.

Thirty-seven years later, we're still here. Same spirit, a few more plants, and a menu that travels from fluffy pancakes to late-night cheeseburgers.
